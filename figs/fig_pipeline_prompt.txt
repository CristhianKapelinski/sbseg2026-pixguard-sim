LDD PROMPT — PixGuard-Sim pipeline banner (paper Figure 1, figs/pipeline.pdf)

Paste the block below into an image model verbatim. It is written to be
deterministic: it specifies the finished diagram rather than a mood, so two
runs should produce essentially the same figure. The four stages match the
four the paper names in Section 3, and every badge number is a real number
from the paper (fifteen schema fields, five detectors, the measured reasoning
latency range).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

FRAME
Ultrawide horizontal banner, aspect ratio 5:1, 3840 x 768 pixels, pure white
background (#FFFFFF). Flat two-dimensional vector artwork only.

ART STYLE AND EXACT PALETTE
Monoline technical wireframe: every shape is a thin outline of uniform 1.5 px
weight with flat fills and no texture. Exactly four colours, each with one role
and no other use:
  - ink navy #1F2A44 — every outline, every arrow, every piece of text.
  - pale slate #E7ECF3 — the fill of every stage panel and every sub-box.
  - soft blue #C3D4E8 — the fill of the data chips that ride the arrows, ONLY.
  - signal amber #E8A33D — the ONE accent colour. It may appear ONLY on the
    clock glyph and the deadline tick inside stage 4, and nowhere else.

NO-LIST (strict)
No gradients. No drop shadows. No glows. No 3D, no isometric or 2.5D
projection, no perspective, no tilted planes. No photorealism. No people, no
faces, no hands. No logos, no brand marks, no flags. No background grid, no
dotted paper, no outer frame around the banner. No full sentences anywhere:
every label is 1 to 3 words or a short number, in a clean geometric sans-serif,
always horizontal.

SCENE SKELETON
Four rectangular stage panels of EQUAL height and equal width, in ONE
horizontal row, left to right, evenly spaced, corners rounded at 8 px. Between
consecutive panels runs a single thin ink-navy arrow pointing right with a
small solid triangular head. One small labelled data chip sits centred on each
of the three arrows, drawn as a tiny rounded rectangle filled soft blue. Three
small cylinders feed stage 1 from the left; they are the ONLY elements off the
main row, stacked vertically at the far left, each joined to stage 1 by its own
short thin arrow.

THE THREE FEEDER SOURCES (far left, off-row, stacked top to bottom)
Each is a small horizontal cylinder outlined ink navy, filled pale slate, with
its name set below it:
  top    — "PixGuard-Sim"
  middle — "Tide HI/LI"
  bottom — "pix-fraud-br"

STAGE 1 — panel title in CAPS at the top of the panel: "ADAPTERS"
Inside: three small stacked rounded sub-boxes, outlined ink navy and filled
pale slate, one per incoming source, converging by short thin arrows into a
single narrow vertical bar on the panel's right edge labelled "one schema". A
floating pill badge overlaps the panel's top-right corner, outlined ink navy on
white, reading "15 fields".

STAGE 2 — panel title in CAPS: "SCENARIO LIBRARY"
Inside: four small rounded sub-boxes in a 2 x 2 arrangement, labelled exactly
"takeover", "mule chain", "MED refund", "coercion". Inside the "MED refund" box
sits a short chain of three tiny circles joined left to right by two tiny
arrows, showing multi-hop layering. The "coercion" box is drawn with a dashed
outline; nothing else in the figure uses a dashed outline.

STAGE 3 — panel title in CAPS: "DETECTOR INTERFACE"
Inside: one wide rounded slot outlined ink navy and filled pale slate, labelled
"pluggable", with a small notch cut into its left edge so it reads as a socket.
Two tiny stacked rounded chips sit to its right, labelled "tabular" and
"graph". A floating pill badge overlaps the panel's top-right corner, outlined
ink navy on white, reading "5 detectors".

STAGE 4 — panel title in CAPS: "DEADLINE SCORER"
Inside: a horizontal gauge bar spanning most of the panel width, outlined ink
navy and filled pale slate, with a single vertical tick in signal amber about
two thirds along it, labelled "deadline" beneath. To the left of the gauge sits
a small clock glyph in signal amber, appearing EXACTLY ONCE in the whole
figure. Below the gauge, two short stacked labels read "in time" and "too
late". A floating pill badge overlaps the panel's top-right corner, outlined
ink navy on white, reading "1066-1979 ms".

THE THREE DATA CHIPS ON THE ARROWS (left to right)
  arrow 1 to 2 — chip reading "events"
  arrow 2 to 3 — chip reading "features"
  arrow 3 to 4 — chip reading "scores + latency"
Each chip appears EXACTLY ONCE and no chip repeats a label used elsewhere.

UNIQUENESS CLAUSES
The clock glyph appears EXACTLY ONCE, in stage 4 only. Signal amber appears
EXACTLY TWICE, on that clock glyph and on the deadline tick. Each of the four
stage panels appears EXACTLY ONCE and none is duplicated or mirrored. Each of
the three feeder cylinders appears EXACTLY ONCE.

COMPOSITION CLOSE
The four stage panels are vertically centred on one common horizontal axis with
generous, equal spacing. Leave at least 8% empty margin on all four sides.
Every label is horizontal; no rotated or vertical text anywhere. The figure
must stay legible and must survive greyscale printing at 15 cm width, so the
fills must differ in lightness and not in hue alone.
