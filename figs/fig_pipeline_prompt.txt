FRAME
Ultrawide horizontal banner, aspect ratio 5:1, 3840 x 768 pixels, pure white
background (#FFFFFF). Flat two-dimensional vector artwork only.

ART STYLE AND EXACT PALETTE
Monoline technical wireframe: every shape is a thin outline of uniform 1.5 px
weight with flat fills and no texture. Exactly four colours, each with one role
and no other use. Light to dark they run sand, teal, vermillion, graphite, and
that ordering must survive a conversion to greyscale:
  - ink graphite #2B2B2B — every outline, every arrow, every piece of text.
  - warm sand #F2EDE4 — the fill of every panel and every sub-box.
  - muted teal #8FB8B3 — the fill of the two settlement bands and of the data
    chips that ride the arrows, ONLY.
  - signal vermillion #D55E00 — the ONE accent colour. It may appear ONLY on
    the clock glyph and the two verdict marks in panel 4, and nowhere else.

NO-LIST (strict)
No gradients. No drop shadows. No glows. No 3D, no isometric or 2.5D
projection, no perspective, no tilted planes. No photorealism. No people, no
faces, no hands. No logos, no brand marks, no flags. No background grid, no
dotted paper, no outer frame around the banner. No full sentences anywhere:
every label is 1 to 3 words or a short number, in a clean geometric sans-serif,
always horizontal.

SCENE SKELETON
Four rectangular panels of EQUAL height, in ONE horizontal row, left to right,
evenly spaced, corners rounded at 8 px. Panel 4 is 1.4 times as wide as each of
the others; panels 1 to 3 are equal in width. Between consecutive panels runs a
single thin graphite arrow pointing right with a small solid triangular head.
One small labelled data chip sits centred on each of the three arrows, drawn as
a tiny rounded rectangle filled muted teal. Three small cylinders feed panel 2
from below; they are the ONLY elements off the main row, sitting in a short
horizontal line beneath panel 2, each joined to it by its own short thin arrow
pointing up.

PANEL 1 — title in CAPS at the top of the panel: "THE WINDOW"
Inside: one horizontal timeline rule spanning the panel width, outlined in
graphite, with a small solid dot at its left end labelled "transfer" beneath,
and a short vertical bar at its right end labelled "irrevocable" beneath. The
stretch of rule between the dot and the bar is filled muted teal as a band. A
floating pill badge overlaps the panel's top-right corner, outlined graphite on
white, reading "300-1999 ms".

PANEL 2 — title in CAPS: "THREE SOURCES"
Inside: three small stacked rounded sub-boxes, outlined graphite and filled
warm sand, converging by short thin arrows into a single narrow vertical bar on
the panel's right edge labelled "one schema". A floating pill badge overlaps
the panel's top-right corner, outlined graphite on white, reading "15 fields".
The three feeder cylinders below the panel are labelled, left to right:
"PixGuard-Sim", "Tide HI/LI", "pix-fraud-br".

PANEL 3 — title in CAPS: "FOUR SCENARIOS"
Inside: four small rounded sub-boxes in a 2 x 2 arrangement, labelled exactly
"takeover", "mule chain", "MED refund", "coercion". Inside the "MED refund" box
sits a short chain of three tiny circles joined left to right by two tiny
arrows. The "MED refund" and "coercion" boxes are drawn with a thicker 3 px
outline than the other two, marking them as the pair no other dataset carries;
nothing else in the figure uses a thick outline. A floating pill badge overlaps
the panel's top-right corner, outlined graphite on white, reading "2 new".

PANEL 4 — title in CAPS: "THE VERDICT"
Inside: one horizontal time ruler spanning the panel width, outlined graphite,
with four short tick marks beneath it labelled, left to right, "0.01", "43",
"1000", "2000", and the word "ms" set once at the ruler's right end. A muted
teal band covers the stretch of ruler from the "1000" tick to just past the
"2000" tick, echoing the band in panel 1. Two vermillion marks sit ON the
ruler: a small filled triangle at the "0.01" tick, labelled "in time" beneath,
and a small filled circle inside the teal band, labelled "too late" beneath. A
small clock glyph in vermillion sits at the ruler's left end, appearing EXACTLY
ONCE in the whole figure. A floating pill badge overlaps the panel's top-right
corner, outlined graphite on white, reading "1066-1979 ms".

THE THREE DATA CHIPS ON THE ARROWS (left to right)
  arrow 1 to 2 — chip reading "deadline"
  arrow 2 to 3 — chip reading "events"
  arrow 3 to 4 — chip reading "scores + latency"
Each chip appears EXACTLY ONCE and no chip repeats a label used elsewhere.

UNIQUENESS CLAUSES
The clock glyph appears EXACTLY ONCE, in panel 4 only. Vermillion appears
EXACTLY THREE TIMES, on that clock glyph and on the two verdict marks. Each of
the four panels appears EXACTLY ONCE and none is duplicated or mirrored. Each
of the three feeder cylinders appears EXACTLY ONCE. The thick 3 px outline
appears EXACTLY TWICE, on the "MED refund" and "coercion" boxes only.

COMPOSITION CLOSE
The four panels are vertically centred on one common horizontal axis with
generous, equal spacing. Leave at least 8% empty margin on all four sides.
Every label is horizontal; no rotated or vertical text anywhere. The figure
must stay legible and must survive greyscale printing at 15 cm width, so the
fills must differ in lightness and not in hue alone.
